Explicit `enter()` or exact `enter(JobMemory&)` creates the actor. Legacy allocator/resolver entry only finds that already admitted thread/fiber record; FLS validates the captured PTD and does not grant admission. The pending-admission stack excludes allocator-handler recursion on the same thread before the actor is published. Admission preparation and JobScope construction occur outside the nonrecursive metadata gate. The original-worker reaping guard rejects same-thread admission during its maintenance without blocking a distinct live child. JobPermit validates exact Parent, actor thread/fiber and live scope; its early thread check rejects asking a foreign live Operation for a permit before validating that other actor's PTD.

Stable ticket rows keep Reserved prefixes private until typed publication. Exact byte/header/map lookups return copied descriptors and pin one ticket for the requesting actor. Temporary pins protect the ticket while compiler borrow metadata is prepared outside the gate; failure reverses only that temporary reservation. The returned typed Map span is itself pinned in concurrent mode after publication. Failure to prepare that metadata retains the already published allocation prefix rather than replaying or guessing cleanup. A ticket's own explicit free removes its current actor's old view, whereas another actor's view or SDK lease rejects free before typed destruction. Actor scope exit drops its views and retires metadata/JobScope outside the Parent gate.

Known failed allocation is classified by this attempt's returned pointer, captured PTD and backing poison state, rather than a global outstanding count that a sibling can legitimately change. Unknown/returned prefixes remain owned and poison admission. Known failed raw free remains Retired and retryable only after confirmed live backing ownership; typed destruction is not repeated. Local LastError guards protect admission, publication, metadata preparation and retirement while preserving the backing allocation/free result. Construction records retain failed resource-bearing prefixes. Close rejects active actors, admissions, views, loans and retained resources without waiting for a live child.

Serialized remains the default, retains its body gate and publishes the existing legacy regions/maps. Only the explicitly selected lower concurrent policy uses authoritative resolver access; invalid policy values reject. **Actual Original Profile/Graph/NativeJobs selection remains disabled.** Ticket pins prevent storage retirement; they do not synchronize concurrent mutation of shared payloads or make upper providers, IO catalogs or business state parallel-safe.
