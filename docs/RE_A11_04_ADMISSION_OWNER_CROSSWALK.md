| B51 donor behavior | Actual product owner | Classification / evidence limit |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Distinct next evidence to require before a behavior PR

- For A11-04, first identify a reachable *current* hook/session/entry boundary where a fresh operation can be admitted after shutdown begins **and** outlive its owner. The product's single DLL worker and its existing tests are the negative controls; do not invent a donor `Entry::Application` to manufacture the failure.
- A useful independent Windows test would deliberately stage a real patched hook callback near disable/retire (before its normal lease acquisition, during an admitted callback, and after a failed quiescence retry), while also checking actual original-call, owner and error behavior. Such a fixture requires controlled scheduling, exact-head MSVC Debug/Release and unload evidence. Merely calling `Binding::close()` with a test lease repeats coverage already present.
- Distinguish the installed MinHook engine transaction, native call lease, external thread scan, session accept gate and the caller's DLL reference. Do not turn a source comparison into a new production lock, custom CRT/ABI, unconditional callback denial or alternative service owner without a failing current-product test.
- Apply Source QA, targeted Windows native Debug/Release, relevant full Native and Production/Golden gates to an eventual executable change. Consumer/game and donor-original behavior remain separate, unproven categories. No existing PR should be merged or reported as fully accepted by this document.
